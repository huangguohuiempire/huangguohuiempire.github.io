RADIANT EMPIRE OF GUOHUI

Official Orbital & Digital Sovereign Portal

The Radiant Empire of Guohui (commonly known as The Radiant Empire or Guohui) is a space-based and digital [[micronation]] established on 6 September 2026 by Emperor Guohui I (Liz Quiehui / Lý Thu Tuệ). The micronation asserts sovereignty over the Geostationary Orbital Position at 105° East longitude (105°E GEO) and its associated space infrastructure, collectively designated as Guohui Station, alongside its sovereign digital domains.

Founder and Monarch

His Imperial Majesty, Emperor Guohui I

The founder and reigning monarch of the Radiant Empire of Guohui is Emperor Guohui I (born Liz Quiehui / Lý Thu Tuệ). The Emperor currently resides and works in Thailand. Highly educated in advanced theoretical and spiritual disciplines, Emperor Guohui I holds a Master of Science in Metaphysics (M.Sc. in Metaphysics) and a Doctor of Theology (Th.D.). The integration of scientific principles, metaphysical insights, and theological philosophy forms the intellectual foundation of the Empire's governance and vision for human expansion into space.

History & Proclamation

Proclamation of Sovereignty

On 6 September 2026, His Imperial Majesty Emperor Guohui I formally issued the Official Proclamation of Sovereignty to All Nations and Peoples of the World. The proclamation declared the establishment of the Radiant Empire of Guohui as a sovereign entity based on natural human rights, scientific self-determination, and progressive principles of international space law.

The foundational decree outlined the nation's core principles, focusing on advanced technological development, artificial intelligence, civil liberty, and peaceful space exploration.

Jurisdiction and Domain

Unlike traditional terrestrial nations, the Empire claims an orbital jurisdiction located at 105°E in Geostationary Orbit. According to the Imperial Government, this positioning allows the state to operate as an orbital and digital sovereign entity, free from terrestrial border conflicts while maintaining a symbolic connection with the East Asian meridian.

The Empire also maintains a sovereign digital presence anchored through its network infrastructure.

Government, Law and Rights

The Radiant Empire of Guohui is structured as an absolute monarchy headed by the reigning monarch, Emperor Guohui I. The state operates under a progressive digital legal framework that guarantees protection of intellectual property, freedom of thought, scientific research, and technological innovation.
